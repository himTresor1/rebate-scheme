# Actual Workflow Stages

## ✅ WORKFLOW STAGES

1. **DRAFT/SUBMITTED** - Application submission by Asset Financier

2. **UNDER_REVIEW** - Analyst Review (includes eligibility criteria evaluation)

3. **qa-review** - QA Team Review

4. **cfo-approval** - CFO Final Approval

5. **APPROVED/REJECTED** - Decision made

6. **FUNDED/disbursed** - Disbursement to beneficiary
