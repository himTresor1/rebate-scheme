## 💡 **FAQ Answers**

**Q: "Can analysts skip the explanation?"**
A: "No - the system validates that explanation is at least 50 characters before allowing approval/rejection. This ensures accountability."

**Q: "What happens when forwarded to M&E?"**
A: "The application enters an M&E investigation queue. M&E team reviews, conducts field verification, and either returns to analyst with findings or makes a final determination."

**Q: "Why both criteria comments AND assessment explanation?"**
A: "Criteria comments are SPECIFIC to each requirement (e.g., 'Income verified via statement #123'). Assessment explanation is OVERALL reasoning (e.g., 'Applicant meets all requirements, recommend approval based on...'). Different levels of detail for different purposes."

**Q: "Can multiple documents be uploaded per criterion?"**
A: "Yes, up to 3 documents per criterion, allowing comprehensive evidence."

**Q: "Is the Additional Motorcycles check mandatory?"**
A: "Yes - it's critical for fraud prevention. The system won't let you save the eligibility check without completing it."
