## 📊 Answer: Who Assigns Applications to Analysts?

**SYSTEM ADMIN** assigns applications to Rebate Analysts through the "Application Manager" component.

**Workflow:**
1. Asset Financier submits application → **Status: PENDING**
2. Admin assigns to Analyst → **Status: ASSIGNED**
3. Analyst reviews → **Status: UNDER-REVIEW**
4. Analyst completes → moves to **QA-REVIEW**
5. QA approves → moves to **CFO-APPROVAL**
6. CFO approves → **Status: APPROVED**
7. Finance disburses → **Status: DISBURSED**
