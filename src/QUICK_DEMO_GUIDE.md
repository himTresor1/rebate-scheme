# 🎯 QUICK DEMO GUIDE - Rebate Analyst Enhancements

## 5-Minute Quick Demo Script

### **Start**: Open any application in Rebate Analyst queue

---

## **Feature 1: Enhanced Eligibility Checker** (1 min)

**Action**: Click blue "Check Eligibility" button in header

**Show**:
1. ✅ **Social Registry Check** → Click "Check Social Registry" → Shows pass/fail + details
2. ✅ **Upload Social Registry Document** → Click "Upload Social Registry Document" → Shows file upload
3. ✅ **Additional Motorcycles Check** → Click "Check Additional Registered Motorcycles" → Shows ALL motorcycles (1-3 bikes with details)
4. ✅ **Optional Document Authentication** → Show National ID/Taxi License verification options

**Say**: *"We now require mandatory Social Registry verification with proof document, plus a motorcycles check to prevent duplicate rebates. Analysts can also optionally authenticate documents via NIDA, RURA, and RRA APIs."*

---

## **Feature 2: Enhanced Criteria** (1 min)

**Action**: Scroll to criteria on right sidebar (or click Review button on mobile)

**Show**:
1. ✅ Mark a criterion YES or NO
2. ✅ Click "Add Comments & Documents" (expand)
3. ✅ Type a comment: *"Income verified via mobile money statement"*
4. ✅ Click "Upload Document" → Select file
5. ✅ Collapse → Show document counter badge

**Say**: *"Each criterion now supports detailed comments and evidence uploads. Analysts can attach up to 3 documents per criterion, creating a complete audit trail."*

---

## **Feature 3: Assessment Section** (1 min)

**Action**: Scroll down past criteria

**Show**:
1. ✅ **Assessment card** with green border
2. ✅ Type in Explanation field: *"After thorough review of all documents and eligibility criteria, the applicant meets all requirements. Income verification confirmed, all mandatory documents authentic."*
3. ✅ Show character counter: "120 / 50 minimum"
4. ✅ Add optional comment: *"Recommend follow-up after 3 months"*

**Say**: *"Analysts MUST explain their reasoning with at least 50 characters. This ensures every decision is documented and accountable."*

---

## **Feature 4: Additional Documents** (30 sec)

**Action**: Scroll to blue "Upload Additional Documents" card

**Show**:
1. ✅ Click "Upload Additional Documents"
2. ✅ Select files (investigation reports, verification certificates)
3. ✅ Show uploaded list with file names and dates

**Say**: *"Analysts can attach documents beyond what the applicant submitted—like investigation reports or verification certificates."*

---

## **Feature 5: Forward to M&E** (1.5 min)

**Action**: Click amber "Forward to M&E" button in header (next to Approve/Reject)

**Show**:
1. ✅ Professional dialog opens with warning notice
2. ✅ Type reason: *"Applicant has discrepancies between Social Registry data and submitted income documents. Requires field verification."*
3. ✅ Check areas of concern:
   - ✅ Income/Financial Discrepancies
   - ✅ Social Registry Data Inconsistencies
4. ✅ Select timeline: **"5 Business Days"**
5. ✅ Add note: *"Request interview with employer to confirm income"*
6. ✅ Click "Forward to M&E Team"

**Say**: *"For edge cases needing investigation, analysts can forward to the M&E team with detailed context. This provides flexibility beyond just approve/reject."*

---

## **Closing** (30 sec)

**Say**: *"These 5 enhancements make the review process more thorough, accountable, and flexible. Every decision is now documented with clear evidence and reasoning, and edge cases can be properly investigated rather than forcing binary decisions."*

---
